The Management Company

Frequently asked questions

Plain answers, in the order people usually ask them.

What is The Management Company?

A confidential forum for venture GPs. Eight to ten managers meet once a month on Zoom, moderated, as the same group over time. The point is repeated interaction, so that members know one another's funds well enough to give useful, candid advice with context.

Who is it for?

Managers of venture funds who have closed a fund and are running it, or raising the next one. We are looking for people who will show up, speak plainly and give as much as they get. It is for GPs only.

Can LPs, founders or service providers join?

No. Membership is for GPs. The room works because everyone in it is making the same kind of decisions, and nobody in it has a stake in your answer.

How is this different from the GP communities I am already in?

Most GPs already know other GPs, and many are in a group chat or two. The difference here is depth and continuity: a small, selected group that stays together, so a peer knows your strategy, your portfolio, your LP base and what you decided last time. We are not a large community, a directory, or a speaker series.

What does a meeting look like?

One Zoom a month, about two hours. The agenda is the members' real issues: fundraising, portfolio, team, and the management company itself. The moderator keeps the structure and adds perspective when it helps. The group does the work. Nobody lectures.

How often do we meet, and is attendance expected?

Monthly, and yes. A room only builds context if the same people keep showing up.

Is there contact between meetings?

Yes. A small private group chat for quick questions and continuity.

Is it really confidential?

What is said in the room stays in the room. The forum exists for issues that are hard to raise with LPs, partners, your team, founders, or in public.

Will this get me introductions to LPs?

No. Membership buys a seat in the room and nothing else. We do not offer LP introductions, placement, fundraising commissions or promises of capital, and we do not coach fundraising. Being a member has no effect on whether we recommend a manager to anyone.

How do you decide who is in?

The room is assembled, not sold, and it is not first come, first served. We read every application by hand, talk with candidates, and invite members personally. We build rooms to avoid direct competitors, because candor suffers when peers compete. We will not fill a seat just because someone is willing to pay.

What happens after I apply?

We read your application. If it looks like a fit, we set up a conversation. If we both think it works, you get a personal invitation. Applying is not a commitment, and nothing is charged when you apply.

What does it cost?

$4,995 per year, paid annually. Founding members, the first group, are locked in at this rate permanently. Most members expense it through their management company as an ordinary business expense.

What if it is not useful?

You have 60 days to get your money back, provided you have attended the first two meetings. Two meetings is enough to see whether the room is right for you.

Is there an in-person event?

There is an annual in-person gathering. It is not part of the first year's commitment.
